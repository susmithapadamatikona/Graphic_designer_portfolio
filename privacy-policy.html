STACKLY / PRIVACY

Your privacy matters.

Information about this demonstration portfolio and how browser data is used.

Data on this device

This is an illustrative portfolio with fictional brands, sample project work and demonstration management tools. It does not provide paid services or secure account access.

Local storage

Project changes, inquiries, favorites, demo profile details and newsletter subscriptions are stored in your browser’s local storage. There is no backend email service or application database. Data is specific to this browser and origin.

External resources

Google Fonts is requested for typography. The hosting provider may process standard request information to serve the site. All project artwork is included locally.

Managing your information

You can remove demo inquiries from the admin area or clear this site’s data in your browser settings. Do not submit sensitive information or real passwords.

Portfolio artwork

The branding artwork and AI-generated product mockups were created for this demonstration. All clients and endorsements shown are fictional.
