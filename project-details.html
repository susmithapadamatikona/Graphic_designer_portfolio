THE CHALLENGE

A distinct voice in a crowded world.

MONO needed an identity that could communicate a clear point of view. The challenge was to move beyond familiar category conventions while remaining approachable, practical and true to the core idea.

The idea. Made visible.

We began with the essentials: a focused message, confident typography and a considered visual system. A small set of adaptable elements brings consistency across every application, from the first impression to the smallest detail.
